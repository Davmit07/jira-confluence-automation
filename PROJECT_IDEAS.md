# Jira and Confluence Automation Ideas

## 1. Weekly Delivery Health Digest

**Problem it solves:** Managers spend time manually assembling project updates and can miss emerging blockers or slipping work. Generate a weekly Confluence summary of progress, upcoming milestones, and risks, with links back to the relevant Jira issues.

**Data needed:** Jira project and sprint details; issue status, priority, assignee, due date, estimates, and blocker or dependency links; plus Confluence project goals, milestone dates, and risk notes.

## 2. At-Risk Milestone Alerts

**Problem it solves:** Work that is blocked, overdue, or trending late may not reach a manager until a milestone is already at risk. Identify at-risk work and notify its owner and manager early.

**Data needed:** Jira issue status, due dates, priority, assignee, status history, and dependency links; Confluence milestone dates, owners, and acceptance criteria.

## 3. Decision-to-Action Follow-Through

**Problem it solves:** Decisions and action items recorded in Confluence meeting notes can be overlooked or never become trackable work. Create or link Jira tasks for agreed actions and flag overdue follow-ups.

**Data needed:** Confluence meeting-note content, page links, dates, and labels; Jira project and issue-type settings, existing issues, assignees, due dates, and action-item status.
