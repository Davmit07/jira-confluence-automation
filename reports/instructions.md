# How to Complete the Weekly Status Report

1. Copy `template.md` and name the copy `weekly-status-YYYY-MM-DD.md`, using the period end date.
2. Fill in the project/team, report owner, and reporting period. Use exact start and end dates.
3. Select one overall status:
   - **Green:** Work is on track; no material issue needs escalation.
   - **Amber:** A risk or dependency could affect a commitment; include the mitigation or decision needed.
   - **Red:** A commitment is blocked or likely to slip; state the impact and escalation needed.
4. Under **Accomplishments**, report completed outcomes and impact from this period. Use specific results or verified metrics where available.
5. Under **Blockers and Risks**, describe the issue, its impact, a named owner, the help or decision needed, and a target resolution date. Write `None` when there are no blockers or material risks.
6. Under **Next Week's Plan**, list concrete deliverables with an owner and target date. Call out dependencies or decisions that could change the plan.
7. Keep bullets concise and factual. Link to relevant Jira issues or project documents when useful; do not include credentials or unnecessary personal data.
8. Before sharing, verify dates, owners, status, metrics, and links, and remove any unused placeholders.
